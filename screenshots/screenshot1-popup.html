Nvidia Corporation

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Nvidia Corporation is an American multinational corporation and technology company headquartered in Santa Clara, California, and incorporated in Delaware. It is a software and fabless company which designs and supplies graphics processing units, application programming interface (APIs) for data science and high-performance computing, and system on a chip (SoCs) for the mobile computing and automotive market.

The company's GPU products are used in gaming, professional visualization, data centers, and automotive markets. Nvidia's compete in the AI chip market with its H100 and upcoming Blackwell architecture.
